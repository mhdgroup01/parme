Parme is a personal finance and point-of-sale (POS) app. This page explains what data we collect, what we use it for, and who we share it with.

1. Data we collect

TypeDetails
AccountEmail, username, full name, password (hashed — we cannot read it)
FinancialIncome/expense records, debts, loans, sales receipts, products, stock, cost and profit
Data about others you enterNames of credit customers, family members, friends
ImagesPayment slips, product photos, shop logo
OtherPush notification tokens, in-app activity logs, IP addresses in server logs

2. How we use it

  • To provide the core service: recording money, selling at the counter, printing receipts, reports
  • To sync your data across your devices and with members of your shop or family group
  • To send order notifications and account emails
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising

3. Data shared with third parties

Important: some features send data out for processing. If you do not use the feature, nothing is sent.

RecipientWhat · when
Google (Gemini)Your voice recording — when you use the microphone button to add a record by speaking
Anthropic (Claude)Your typed message plus a summary of your balances — when you use the AI assistant
GoogleIdentity verification — if you choose Sign in with Google
CloudflareBot protection — at sign-in
ResendAccount and password-reset emails

4. Where we store it, and for how long

  • Data is stored on our servers in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur)
  • Kept while your account is active — when you request account deletion, we remove all data within 30 days
  • Some data also stays on your own device so the app works offline

5. Your rights

  • View and edit your data inside the app
  • Export your data as CSV or Excel from the reports screen
  • Delete data yourself: in the app → Settings → Danger zone → “Delete all data” (removes income/expense records, debts, loans, trips and settings)
  • Delete your whole account: email mhdgroup01@gmail.com from your registered address — we will delete your account, shop (POS) data, family data and images within 30 days

6. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. The database enforces Row Level Security so each person can only reach their own data, or data of a shop or family group they belong to.

7. Children

Parme is built for adults running a business. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If we make a significant change we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app.
