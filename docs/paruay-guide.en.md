# Parme User Guide

Parme is an all-in-one money app — personal income/expense tracking, splitting bills and lending with friends, family/group finances, a point-of-sale (POS) shop system, group trips, and even games. This article walks through every feature, topic by topic, so new users can get started right away.

## 1. Personal Finance

### 💰 Balance & Logging Transactions

The home screen shows your current balance, with quick buttons to log income and expenses on the spot.

**Balance Card** — The large card at the very top shows your total balance (income minus expenses), along with today's income and expense totals. If your balance goes negative, the number turns red and a 'Negative' tag appears as a warning. If you use foreign currencies, each currency's balance is shown separately below the main total. How to: Open the app and you'll see the balance card right at the top → Check your current balance in the middle of the card → Look at the 'Today' section below to see how much came in and went out today. *(Tip: If the balance is red with a warning tag, it means your expenses have exceeded your income, so it's a good time to review your spending)*

**Add Income** — Log money you've received, such as your salary, sales, or a refund. How to: Tap the green 'Income' button below the balance card → Enter the amount → Pick a category, or type your own label in the field at the top → Add extra details if you like (optional), and pick a date if it isn't today → Tap 'Save'. *(Tip: If you'd rather not pick a category, just type your own label in the 'Custom label' field — the app will use that name instead of a category)*

**Add Expense** — Log money you've spent, like food, transport, or everyday purchases. How to: Tap the red 'Expense' button below the balance card → Enter the amount and pick a category → Add a note or attach a photo (like a receipt) if you want → Tap 'Save'. *(Tip: Attach a receipt photo using the camera button or by picking from your gallery — handy for checking your records later)*

**Edit or Delete a Transaction** — Fix the amount, note, or date of a transaction, or remove one you logged by mistake. How to: Scroll down to the list of income and expenses → Tap the pencil icon next to a transaction to edit it → Adjust the amount, note, date, or category, then save → To remove it, tap the delete button and confirm when asked 'Delete this transaction?'. *(Tip: The list has a search box — type a category, note, or date to find older transactions faster)*

### 🏷️ Income & Expense Categories

The app comes with ready-made categories from the very first launch, and you can customize them to fit your life.

**Default Categories** — On first use, the app automatically creates a standard set of income and expense categories, each with its own icon, so you can start logging right away without setting anything up first. How to: Open the income or expense logging screen → Existing categories appear as icon buttons to choose from → Tap the category you want before saving. *(Tip: Income and expenses have separate sets of categories — pick the transaction type first to see the right ones)*

**Edit / Add Your Own Categories** — Add new categories, rename them, or change their icons to match how you actually spend. How to: Go to Settings → Find the 'Category Settings' section → Tap 'Add Category' to create a new one, or edit the name/icon of an existing one → Remove any categories you don't use. *(Tip: Keep categories detailed enough to be useful but not too many — it keeps your pie chart easy to read and gives you a clearer picture of your spending)*

### 🎯 Budgets & Goals

Set spending budgets and income goals to keep your money in check. The app keeps you posted and shows your progress clearly.

**Monthly Budget + Daily Budget** — Set a monthly spending limit and the app automatically works out an average daily budget, showing a progress bar with the percentage used and how much you have left or have gone over. How to: Go to Settings and open 'Monthly Budget' → Enter your monthly budget amount (enter 0 to turn it off) → Save, then return to the home screen to see the budget card with its progress bar → Check the 'Daily Budget' line on the card to see whether you've gone over your average daily allowance today. *(Tip: The daily budget is your monthly budget divided by the number of days in the month — it makes day-to-day spending easier to manage)*

**Alerts When You're Near or Over Budget** — When you've spent 80% of your budget, the bar turns a warning color (almost out), and once you pass 100% an 'Over budget!' tag appears showing how much you've overspent — for both the monthly and daily budgets. How to: Set a monthly budget first → Log expenses as usual → Watch the budget bar's color: green is fine, orange/yellow means you're close (80%), and red means you're over budget. *(Tip: The warning tags work for both the monthly and daily budgets. If you see an 'Over budget today' tag, it means you've exceeded your average daily allowance for the day)*

**Daily + Monthly Income Goals** — Set how much income you want to bring in each day or month. The app shows a progress card and displays 'Goal reached' once you hit it — great for shop owners or anyone with steady incoming earnings. How to: Go to Settings and open 'Daily Goal' and/or 'Monthly Goal' → Enter your target income amount (enter 0 to turn it off) → Save, and the goal card will appear on the home screen with a progress bar. *(Tip: The daily goal counts only today's income, while the monthly goal counts your total income for the whole month)*

**Savings Goal (Main Goal)** — Set a lump sum you want to save up, like 'Buy a new car.' The app names your goal and shows a progress bar based on how much your balance has grown toward the target, displaying 'Goal reached' when you make it. How to: Go to Settings and open the 'Savings Goal' section → Name your goal (e.g. Buy a new car) and enter the amount you want → Save, and the savings card will appear on the home screen with a progress percentage. *(Tip: Progress is measured by how much your balance has grown since you set the goal — the more you save, the fuller the bar gets)*

### 📊 Charts & Statistics

Get an overview of your finances through a cash flow chart over time and a pie chart showing where your money goes.

**Cash Flow Chart** — Shows money in and out over time as lines or bars. View it several ways: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or all-time, so you can spot when you're earning or spending more. How to: Scroll down to the 'Cash Flow' section → Tap a time range: Day / Week / Month / Year / All → Read the chart to see your income and expense trends for that period. *(Tip: Pick 'Month' or 'Year' for the long-term picture, and 'Day' to see your most recent activity)*

**Pie Chart by Category** — Shows the share of money in each category as a pie. Switch between three views: All (income and expenses combined), income only, or expenses only — so you can see where most of your money goes or comes from. How to: Scroll to the pie chart → Tap a mode toggle: 'All' / 'Income' / 'Expense' → Look at each slice and its percentage for each category. *(Tip: In 'All' mode, each category is marked with an arrow showing whether it's money in (↑) or money out (↓), so you can compare them in a single chart)*

### 💱 Currencies & Exchange Rates

Supports multiple currencies — perfect if you hold foreign cash or make cross-currency transactions.

**Main Currency** — Choose the main currency used to display all balances in the app (the default is the Lao kip ₭). Pick from a list of currencies worldwide. How to: Go to Settings and open the 'Currency' section → Tap the 'Main Currency' box → Pick the currency you want from the list. *(Tip: The main currency is what's used to calculate your total balance and all your budgets)*

**Secondary Currencies (add as many as you need)** — Add foreign currencies you use often so you can log transactions directly in them. Each currency's balance is shown separately on the balance card. How to: In the Currency section, scroll to 'Secondary Currencies' → Tap 'Add Currency' and pick the one you want → Remove any you don't use with the X button → When logging a transaction, you can choose a secondary currency on the entry screen. *(Tip: Secondary currencies are optional — only add what you really need to keep your screen uncluttered)*

**Exchange Rates (automatic + custom)** — The app automatically pulls the latest exchange rates (mid-market) every day to convert secondary currencies into your main one. If you'd rather use your own rate, you can just type a number over it. How to: In the Currency section, scroll to 'Exchange Rates' → Tap the 🔄 refresh button to load the latest rates (the update date is shown) → Leave a field blank to use the automatic rate, or type your own number to set a custom rate. *(Tip: If it says 'Rates not loaded yet,' tap 🔄 first. Any field you leave blank will use the automatic rate)*

## 2. Social Finance (Friends · Split Bills · Lending · IOU Inbox · Notifications)

### 👥 Friends and Friend Requests

Before you can split a bill or lend money to anyone in the app, you'll need to add them as a friend first. Here's how to find friends, send and accept requests, and give them nicknames that are easy to remember.

**Add a Friend (by username or 6-digit code)** — There are two ways to add a friend: search by their username, or enter the 6-digit friend code they share with you. Once you find them, send a request. They'll need to accept before you're connected. How to: Open the Friends page and go to the '+ Add Friend' tab → Type a username (at least 2 characters) and tap search, or enter a 6-digit code in the 'Add by code' field → Tap '➕ Send Request' next to their name → Wait for them to accept — you'll see a '⏳ Pending' status in the meantime. *(Tip: If your search comes up empty, the app will show 'User not found' — double-check that the username is spelled exactly right)*

**Share Your Friend Code** — Everyone has their own 6-digit friend code. Share yours so others can add you instantly without having to type your name. How to: Open the Friends page and find the 'My Friend Code' section → Tap 'Copy' — you'll see '✓ Copied' → Send the code to your friend by chat or any way you like. *(Tip: This code is safe to share — it only lets people send you a friend request, nothing more)*

**Requests Received / Sent (Accept or Decline)** — The 'Requests' tab gathers invites others have sent you (received) and the ones you've sent that are still waiting (sent). You can accept or decline any incoming request. How to: Open the Friends page and go to the 'Requests' tab → Under 'Received', tap '✓ Accept' to become friends or '✗ Decline' to dismiss it → Invites you've sent appear under 'Sent' with a 'Pending' status, and you can cancel them. *(Tip: If there's nothing here, the app will simply show 'No requests')*

**Give a Friend a Nickname** — Rename a friend to something easier to remember — turn a long username into 'Mom' or 'Roomie', for example. The nickname only shows on your own device and doesn't affect anything on their end. How to: In your friends list, tap the friend you want to rename → Choose 'Edit Name' and type the nickname you want → To go back to their original name, leave it blank or tap 'Reset'. *(Tip: Nicknames are saved only on this device — you'll need to set them again if you switch phones)*

### 🧾 Splitting Bills with Friends

Perfect for meals out, trips, or group purchases. Split the whole bill evenly, or break it down item by item so everyone pays for what they got. Then send a request for your friends to confirm their share.

**Turn On Split Mode and Add People** — Start a split by flipping on the 'Split with Friends' switch, then add everyone who's chipping in. You can pick from your real friends list or just type names in by hand. How to: Open the 'Split Bill' menu → Turn on the '🤝 Split with Friends' switch → Add people one at a time, or tap '📒 Pick from Friends' to pull names from your friends list → Give each person a name if they don't have one yet. *(Tip: Choose 'Link to Account' to connect someone in the bill to a real friend in the app — that way you can send a confirmation request and track what they owe)*

**Add Items to the Bill (with photos and prices)** — Instead of splitting one lump sum, you can add individual items like 'Salad' or 'Drinks' with their prices, attach photos, and choose who's chipping in for each one. How to: On the split bill screen, tap '+ Add Item' → Enter the item name and price (attach a photo if you like) → Tap to choose who's paying for that item (everyone is selected by default) → Turn on 'Use item total' if you want the bill amount calculated automatically from all the items. *(Tip: When you add a new person to the bill, the app automatically includes them in every item. If they didn't get something, just tap to remove them from it)*

**Split Evenly and See Each Person's Share** — Choose to split the whole bill evenly, and the app works out 'Your Share' and each friend's amount, with a per-person summary. How to: Choose 'Split Evenly' to divide the total equally among everyone → Check 'Your Share' and the summary for each friend → Review all the numbers before saving. *(Tip: If someone didn't join in on certain items, break those out as separate items instead of splitting the whole bill evenly — it's fairer that way)*

**Save the Bill and Send Confirmation Requests** — When you save, the app records the bill for you. If it's linked to real friends, it sends them a confirmation request. They need to accept before the IOU amounts take effect in both your accounts. How to: Once the amounts look right, tap 'Split Bill' to save → If it's linked to friends, tap 'Send Confirmation Request' → You'll see '✓ Split bill saved' and a '⏳ Awaiting confirmation' status until your friend responds. *(Tip: You can also text the invoice to a friend with the '📤 Send Invoice' button — handy if they're not using the app yet)*

### 💵 Lending and Loans (with Interest)

Keep track of money you lend to friends. You can charge interest — either simple or compound — and the app works out the principal plus interest as time goes by. You can also send the loan to your friend to confirm.

**Create a Loan and Set the Interest** — Enter the principal, then set an interest rate as a percentage along with a time period (day/week/month/year). The app uses these to calculate the amount owed. How to: Open the 'Lend' menu → Enter the principal amount and choose who's borrowing (tap '📒 Pick from Friends') → Enter the 'Interest' as a percentage and choose a period: day / week / month / year → Tap save — you'll see '✓ Loan saved'. *(Tip: Don't want to charge interest? Just set it to 0 and the app will track the principal only)*

**Choose Simple or Compound Interest** — Simple interest is always based on the principal alone, while compound interest adds the interest back to the balance each period — so the amount grows faster over longer stretches of time. How to: On the loan details screen, choose 'Interest Type' → Pick 'Simple' for straightforward interest, or 'Compound' for interest that builds on itself → The app recalculates the amount automatically based on your choice. *(Tip: For most short-term loans, 'Simple' is easier to understand and fairer. 'Compound' suits longer-term loans)*

**Track Principal + Interest Over Time** — The app counts the days since you made the loan and automatically calculates the total owed (principal plus accrued interest) every time you check — no math required on your end. How to: Tap the loan you want to view → Check 'Principal + Interest' for the total owed as of today, and 'Accrued Interest' for the interest that has built up → You can also see how many days/periods have passed. *(Tip: The interest grows on its own as time passes — the longer the repayment takes, the higher it climbs, with no manual updates needed)*

**Record Payments and Close the Loan** — When your friend pays back part or all of the loan, record it and the app deducts it from the balance. Once it's fully paid, you can close the account. How to: Open the loan and record the amount your friend paid back → See the remaining balance drop after you save → Once it's paid in full, tap 'Close Account' — the loan will show 'Paid in Full'. *(Tip: Record every payment you receive, even small ones — the app keeps your balance accurate)*

**Send a Loan Request for Your Friend to Confirm** — If it's linked to a real friend, you can send a loan request. They need to accept before the loan is recorded in both accounts. How to: On the loan, tap 'Link to Account' and choose a friend → Tap 'Send Loan Request' → Wait for the '⏳ Awaiting confirmation' status until your friend accepts — it'll change to '✓ Accepted'. *(Tip: While you're waiting, if you picked the wrong person, tap 'Change Friend' to resend the request to the right one)*

### 📬 IOU Inbox and Incoming Loan Requests

When a friend splits a bill or sets up a loan with you, the request lands in this inbox. You have to accept it before anything is added to your account — so no one can put a debt on you without your agreement.

**IOU Request Inbox (from split bills)** — Collects the IOU requests friends have sent for you to confirm. Each one shows who it's from, the amount, plus a note and date. You can accept or decline. How to: Open the '📬 IOU Request Inbox' (reachable from the Split Bill button with the red number) → Read the details: from / amount / note / date → Tap '✓ Accept' to add the IOU to your account, or '✗ Decline' to dismiss it. *(Tip: Once you accept, the IOU shows up in your account right away. If it doesn't match what you agreed on, decline it and talk to your friend first)*

**Incoming Loan Requests** — When a friend sets up a loan with you as the borrower, the request comes in with the principal, interest, and time period so you can decide whether to accept. How to: Open the request inbox and find '💰 Loan Request' → Check the principal / interest / period against what you agreed on → Tap '✓ Accept Loan' to record it in your account, or '✗ Decline'. *(Tip: Look over the interest rate and period carefully before accepting — the amount grows over time just as it does on the lender's side)*

### 🔔 Reminders, Notifications, and Menu Badges

This section helps you stay on top of money owed and never miss what's happening — sending friends gentle reminders, confirming payments received, and the red badges on menu buttons that flag anything waiting for you.

**Send a Reminder (nudge a friend)** — Send a friend a gentle reminder about an outstanding debt. Write your own message or use a ready-made one, and they'll get a notification. How to: Open an outstanding IOU and tap '🔔 Remind' → Type your own message or use a suggested one like 'Hey, you still owe me 😊' → Tap 'Send' — you'll see '✓ Reminder sent'. *(Tip: Reminding too soon shows '⏱ You just sent a reminder' — give it a little time before nudging again)*

**Mark as Paid and Confirm Receipt** — The borrower taps 'Paid' to send a confirmation request to the lender. The lender then confirms they received it, or disputes that they haven't. Once both sides confirm, the IOU closes in both accounts. How to: Borrower: on the IOU, tap '✓ Paid (send confirmation request)' → Lender: open the '💰 Payment Notice' notification and tap '✓ Confirm Received' or '✗ Not Received Yet' → Once confirmed, you'll see '🎉 IOU paid in full — recorded in both accounts'. *(Tip: Don't delete the entry before the other side confirms — that confirmation is what keeps the balances matching on both devices)*

**In-App Notification Center** — One place for everything happening — both requests (📬) and notifications (🔔), like reminders from friends, messages, payment notices, and confirmations. You can mark items as read and clear them out. How to: Open the notification center and switch between the '📬 Requests' and '🔔 Notifications' tabs → Tap an item to handle it (confirm/respond) depending on the notification type → Tap 'Mark as Read' or 'Clear Read' to tidy up. *(Tip: If it's empty, you'll see 'No notifications yet' — nothing to worry about, it just means nothing's pending)*

**Red Badges on Menu Buttons (Split Bill / Lend)** — Menu buttons show a red number badge when there are requests waiting for you. The 'Split Bill' button shows how many IOU requests are pending, and the 'Lend' button shows pending loan requests. The Accounts button rolls it all up, so you can spot it at a glance without digging in. How to: Check the 'Split Bill' button — the red number is how many IOU requests are awaiting confirmation → Check the 'Lend' button — the red number is how many loan requests are waiting → Tap any button with a red badge to handle the pending requests. *(Tip: The numbers update in real time — once you've handled a request, the red badge disappears right away)*

## 3. Family/Group & Group Trips (Parme)

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family / Group

Pool everyone's income and expenses into one shared wallet and see the group's combined balance together. Perfect for families, roommates, or small teams.

**Create a group or join with an invite code** — Start your own brand-new group, or join an existing one by entering the invite code a friend shares with you. How to: Open the Family / Group page → Tap Create Family, give the group a name, then tap Create → To join a friend's group, tap Join with Code, paste the invite code you received, then tap Join → Whoever creates the group automatically becomes the owner. *(Tip: The invite code has to match exactly, otherwise you'll get an 'invalid code' message)*

**Share the invite code to add members** — The group info box shows your invite code with a copy button. Send it to friends so they can join the same group. How to: Open the group you want → Look for the Invite Code box near the top → Tap Copy (you'll see 'Copied') → Send the code to friends via chat or messaging app so they can join. *(Tip: You can see the member count and who the owner is right on the group page)*

**Add shared income and expenses** — Everyone in the group can log income or expenses into the same shared pot, and the group total updates for everyone in real time. How to: On the group page, tap Add Income or Add Expense → Enter the amount and pick a category (or type your own label) → Add extra details if you like (optional) and attach a photo → Tap Save and the entry instantly rolls into the group total. *(Tip: Check the Group Total to see the combined balance, and filter by day, week, month, year, or all time)*

**Group chat** — Chat right inside the group. Reply to messages, and edit or delete your own. You'll also see when someone's typing. How to: Open the Chat tab in the group → Type a message and tap Send → Long-press a message to Reply, Edit, or Delete. *(Tip: Handy for sorting out shared expenses without ever leaving the app)*

**Set group goals and budgets** — Set income goals and spending budgets for the group, daily and monthly, plus a main savings goal that keeps building up without resetting. How to: Tap Set Goals on the group page → Enter a monthly income goal and a monthly spending budget (and a daily goal if you want) → Set a Main Goal for long-term savings that doesn't reset each month → Tap Save. *(Tip: The app clearly flags when you've Hit Your Goal or when you're Over Budget or Close to the Limit)*

**Manage, leave, or delete the group** — Leave a group when you no longer want to be in it, or, as the owner, delete it permanently for everyone, including wiping all entries. How to: Open the group's Settings → Tap Leave This Group to leave on your own (you'll be asked to confirm) → To delete it for good, tap Delete This Group Permanently and type Yes to confirm → You can also set up shared categories and choose the group's currency on this page. *(Tip: Permanently deleting the group or clearing all entries requires typing Yes to confirm, because it removes everything for every member and can't be undone)*

### 🧳 Group Trips

Track expenses while traveling together. The app keeps tabs on who paid for what and who split with whom, then works out exactly who owes who in the end, with a clean two-step payment confirmation.

**Create a new trip** — Start a trip for a single journey, give it a name, and add a note. Whoever creates it becomes the trip owner. How to: Open the Group Trips page → Tap + New Trip → Name the trip (e.g. Chiang Mai Trip 2026) and add a note if you like → Tap Create and the trip shows up under Active trips. *(Tip: Finished trips can be moved to the Closed group to keep things tidy)*

**Invite friends to the trip** — Invite people from your friends list to join the trip. They'll get an invitation and can accept or decline. How to: Open the trip and go to the Members tab → Tap + Invite Friend and pick friends from your list → Your friends will see the invite on their trip page and can Join or Decline → Each member shows as Pending, Joined, or Declined. *(Tip: You have to be friends in the app before you can invite someone, so add them from the main page first if you haven't)*

**Log trip expenses and split them** — Record every bill on the trip: who paid, how much, and who it's split with. Split evenly or only among certain people. How to: In the trip, go to the Expenses tab and tap + Add Expense → Choose who paid and enter the amount → Choose who to split with (check Split Evenly, or pick just the people involved) → Tap Save; you can edit any entry later. *(Tip: Check your own summary under Total Spent, My Share, I Paid, and My Balance to see whether you're owed money or still owe some)*

**Settle up and see who owes who** — The Balance tab automatically calculates and suggests payments, showing who owes who and how much, all arranged to keep transfers to a minimum. How to: Open the trip and go to the Balance tab → Check the Suggested Payments section, which shows who owes who and how much → Whoever owes money transfers it for real, then uses the in-app button to record the payment. *(Tip: Once everyone's even, it'll say Settled with nothing left to pay)*

**Confirm payments (two-step)** — After the money is transferred, the system confirms it from both sides: the payer marks it as sent, then the recipient confirms they actually got it before the balance clears. How to: Whoever owes money taps I've Transferred after sending it; the system shows Waiting for confirmation → The recipient gets a notification and opens the Balance tab → The recipient taps Confirm Received and confirms the amount they got → The entry is logged as paid on both sides. *(Tip: While waiting, you'll see a Pending Confirmation label. If you marked it by mistake, you can Cancel the Request before the other person confirms)*

## 4. Store POS (Storefront / Restaurant)

### 🏪 Getting Started: Set Up Your Store and Add Products

Before you can start selling, set up your store and add your products. Everything happens in one app, with nothing extra to install.

**Open a store / Choose which store to sell from** — Everyone automatically gets their own 'My Store.' If a friend invites you to sell with them, you'll have several stores to switch between, and the app remembers the last one you used. How to: Tap the 'Sell (POS)' button on the home screen → Your current store's name appears at the top — tap it to switch to 'My Store' or '(Friend)'s Store' → View your 'Joined Stores' in Settings, and tap the x to leave any store you no longer want. *(Tip: Each store keeps its sales separate, so always make sure you've picked the right store before you start selling)*

**Add and edit products** — Create products with their price, cost, stock count, photo, and category so you can ring them up fast and let the app calculate profit for you automatically. How to: Go to the 'Products' tab and tap 'Add Product' → Enter the product name and price (required) → Add cost and stock if you like (optional — leave stock blank for unlimited) → Add a photo or emoji and assign a category → Tap an existing product anytime to 'Edit Product'. *(Tip: Once you add a cost, the app calculates profit for you on the sales screen. And if you set a stock count, it warns you when stock is running low and shows 'Out' when an item sells out)*

**Organize products into categories** — Group products into categories like Food, Drinks, or Desserts so they're easy to find while selling, and use them to filter your view. How to: On the Products tab, tap '+ Add' next to the category heading → Name the category and pick an emoji for it → While selling, tap a category name at the top to view just that category (or 'All'). *(Tip: Deleting a category doesn't delete its products — they simply move to 'No category')*

### 🧾 Selling and Taking Payment

The heart of your everyday workflow: add items to the cart, take payment, and hand the customer a receipt.

**Sell and take payment (cart)** — Tap products to add them to the cart, let the app total everything up, then close the sale to record it and update your stock. How to: Go to the 'Sell' tab and tap the items the customer is buying (tap again to add more) → Check the 'Total' in the cart at the bottom → Pick the salesperson (if you have multiple staff) and add a discount if needed → Tap 'Pay' to record the sale — you'll see 'Sale recorded' and your stock updates automatically. *(Tip: You can apply a discount as a percentage (5/10/15/20%) or as a fixed amount, and the app shows the discounted total before you confirm)*

**Receipts + payment QR + store logo** — Set up your store details (name, phone, logo) and add a bank or LINE QR code to your bills. After a sale, you can print the receipt or save it as an image for the customer. How to: Go to Settings and tap 'Store Info (for receipts)' → Enter your store name and phone number, and add a logo → Add a payment QR image (bank QR or LINE) so customers can scan to pay → After a sale, a 'Saved — print or download?' window lets you choose 'Print' or 'Save Image'. *(Tip: Set up your store info once and it's used on every bill. You can also reopen past bills from the sales screen to reprint them)*

**Link sales to your main finances** — Choose whether your POS sales count toward income in your main finances or stay in a separate account. How to: Go to Settings and find 'Link to main finances' → On = sales count as income in your main balance → Off = the POS keeps its own books and doesn't affect your main balance. *(Tip: If you want to keep your store's books separate from your personal money, leave this off)*

### 🍽️ Restaurants: Tables and Open Bills

If you run a restaurant or cafe, use the table system to keep bills open, let customers keep ordering, and settle the whole bill when they leave.

**Set the number of tables** — Tell the app how many tables your place has, and it shows a table map so you can see which ones are free or occupied. How to: Go to Settings and enter the 'Number of tables' (the store owner sets this) → Enter 0 = no tables (regular over-the-counter selling) → Go to the 'Tables' tab to see the table map, free or occupied. *(Tip: For a regular shop with no tables, set this to 0 and just use the normal sell screen)*

**Open a bill by table or by name** — Keep a bill open at a table so customers can keep ordering and the total builds up until they're ready to pay. Besides tables, you can also open a bill under a name. How to: On the Tables tab, tap a free table to open a new bill → Or tap '+ New Bill' and give it a name (like the customer's name) if you're not using tables → Keep adding items to the bill — the total builds up on that bill → When the customer is ready to leave, open their bill and tap Pay to close it out. *(Tip: Closing a bill clears its items, so the app asks you to confirm first. Once a table is paid, it goes back to 'Free')*

**Move tables / merge bills** — Move customers to another table, or merge two bills when groups sit together. How to: Open the menu for the table you want and tap 'Move/Merge Table' → Choose the destination — a free table = move, a table with an existing bill = merge → Confirm when the app asks. *(Tip: If you use QR ordering, customers need to scan the new table's QR after a move (the old code won't work anymore))*

**Service charge** — Automatically add a percentage service charge to table bills — handy for places that charge for service. How to: Turn on the service charge in Settings and set the percentage (default is 10%) → When you sell through a table bill, the app adds the service charge automatically → The service charge appears as its own line on the bill and receipt. *(Tip: The service charge is calculated after any discount, and only applies to table bills)*

### 📱 Customer Self-Ordering by QR + Kitchen/Bar

Let customers scan a QR code at their table and order from their phones. Orders pop up for your staff to confirm, and you can print tickets to the kitchen or bar.

**Per-table QR for customer self-ordering** — Each table has its own QR code. How to: Open the table map and tap a table to show its QR code → Stick the QR on the table, or let customers scan it from your screen → Customers pick items, set quantities, add notes (like 'not spicy'), and tap to send the order. *(Tip: A table's QR code stays valid until the bill is settled. **Accept or reject customer orders** — Customer orders don't go straight onto the bill — they pop up for staff to review first. How to: When a new order comes in, you'll hear an alert and see a 'New order from customer' popup with the table number → Review the items and the customer's notes, adjusting quantities (+/−) if needed → Tap 'Confirm to table' to add it to that table's bill, or 'Reject' to turn it down. *(Tip: If several orders are waiting, the app shows how many are left so you can confirm them one at a time)*

**Call a server / request the bill from the table** — Customers can call a server or ask for the bill right from the QR screen, and the store gets a sound and message alert with the table number. How to: The customer taps 'Call Server' or 'Request Bill' on the ordering screen → The store gets an alert like 'Table 3 · Server requested' → Staff head over to the table. *(Tip: The call button has a short cooldown to stop repeat taps (it can be used again after 30 seconds))*

**Kitchen / bar system (print tickets by station)** — Split orders and print tickets to the kitchen and bar by product type, with customer notes included, so the kitchen and bar can work in parallel without mix-ups. ### 🔐 Install, Accounts & Data Security

This section covers the behind-the-scenes stuff: installing the app to your home screen, signing in to back up your data to the cloud, and real-time syncing.

**Install the app to your home screen (PWA)** — Add Parme to your phone's home screen so it opens fast and works like a real app — no need to visit a website every time. How to: On the Settings page, find the "Install as App" section → Tap the "📱 Install" button → On iPhone: tap the Share button, then choose "Add to Home Screen" → On Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then choose "Install App". *(Tip: Once installed, the Settings page shows "✓ Installed" in place of the install button)*

**Sign in / create an account** — Sign in to back up your data to the cloud and play games or share with friends. You can sign in with Google, or register with a username/email and password. How to: When you first open the app, you'll see a welcome screen → Tap "Sign in with Google," or enter your username/email and password → Don't have an account yet? Tap "Sign Up" and set a password (at least 6 characters) → The first time, you may be asked for your birthday and gender (just once). *(Tip: You can see which account you're signed in to on the Settings page, and tap "Sign Out" anytime. Note: if you open the app directly from a file, Google sign-in won't work — you'll need to open it through a web link)*

**Real-time sync + offline use** — Your data syncs to the cloud automatically and updates instantly with every change, while a copy is kept on your device so you can still view everything if your connection drops. How to: Just use the app normally — your data syncs on its own whenever you're online → If your connection drops, the latest data saved on your device is still viewable → When you're back online, the app pulls in the newest data automatically → If a sync fails, a "Retry" button appears for you to tap. *(Tip: On top of instant updates, the app also auto-refreshes every 9 seconds as a backup, so nothing slips through if an instant update is ever missed)*

**Data security** — Your data is tied to your account, and others only see what you share (like an IOU or a game invite). Deleting all your data requires typing a confirmation word to prevent accidental deletion. How to: Your income and expense data is private and tied to your signed-in account → To delete everything, go to the "Danger Zone" and tap "🗑️ Delete All Data" → Type the word "Yes" to confirm (the button only works once you do) → Confirm once more before it's permanently deleted. *(Tip: Deleting all data can't be undone — it removes all your income, expenses, IOUs, loans, trips, friends, and notifications. Only use it when you're completely sure)*
